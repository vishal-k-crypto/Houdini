Stuck Detection Logic

flowchart TB
    A["🎯 Current Macro Step"] --> B{"Same step as last attempt?"}
    B -->|Yes| C["📈 Increment step_attempt_count"]
    B -->|No| D["🔄 Reset step_attempt_count = 1"]
    C --> E{"attempt_count > 5?"}
    D --> F["⚙️ Execute Macro Step"]
    E -->|Yes| G["🔍 Verify if step already complete"]
    E -->|No| F
    G -->|Complete| H["✅ Advance to next step"]
    G -->|Not Complete| I["⏭️ Force skip stuck step"]
    F --> J{"Needs supervisor?"}
    J -->|Yes| K["👨‍💼 Get Supervisor Guidance"]
    J -->|No| L{"Step complete?"}
    K --> M{"Guidance type?"}
    M -->|abort| N["❌ Abort"]
    M -->|skip| O["⏭️ Skip step"]
    M -->|new_actions| P["🎬 Execute new actions"]
    L -->|Yes| Q["✅ Advance to next step"]
    L -->|No| R["🔄 Retry step"]
